Clause 03

Donations & Payments

All donations made through Hopewell are voluntary and, except as described under Refunds & Cancellations, non-refundable. Payments are processed by PCI-compliant third-party processors; Hopewell does not store full card numbers on its own servers.

Recurring ("monthly") donations continue automatically until cancelled by the donor from their account dashboard or by written request to hello@hopewell.org. Currency conversion and processor fees, where applicable, are disclosed at checkout before you confirm payment.

100% of your donation is allocated to the cause you select; a separate, optional "cover our fees" contribution funds platform operations.

Refunds & Cancellations

If you believe a donation was made in error — duplicate charge, incorrect amount, or unauthorized transaction — contact us within 14 days of the charge. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

Because funds may be disbursed to field partners quickly, refund requests made after a campaign has closed or after funds have shipped to a partner NGO will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis and are not guaranteed.
