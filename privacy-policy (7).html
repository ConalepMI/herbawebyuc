Section 01

Overview

Hopewell Foundation ("Hopewell," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This Policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit our website, donate, register as a volunteer, or contact us, and how that information is used, shared, and protected.

This Policy works alongside our Terms & Conditions. By using our Services, you agree to the practices described here.

Section 02

Information We Collect

We collect information in three ways: what you give us directly, what's generated automatically as you browse, and what verified partners share with us about program delivery.

CategoryExamplesSource
Identity & contactName, email, phone, mailing addressProvided by you at checkout or signup
Donation detailsAmount, cause selected, frequency, receipt historyGenerated when you donate
Payment tokensCard type, last 4 digits, processor tokenProvided by our payment processor
Volunteer detailsSkills, availability, background-check statusProvided by you during registration
Usage dataPages visited, device type, approximate locationCollected automatically via cookies
Section 03

How We Use Information

  • Process donations, issue tax receipts, and manage recurring gifts.
  • Send impact updates, receipts, and donor communications you've opted into.
  • Match and coordinate volunteers with field opportunities.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized transactions.
  • Improve the website and understand which causes resonate with donors.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use donation data to train third-party advertising models.

Section 04

How We Share Information

We share the minimum information necessary with:

  • Payment processors — to complete and secure your transaction.
  • Verified NGO partners — aggregate, non-identifying delivery data only (e.g., "500 meals funded"), never your personal contact details, unless you opt in to a partner-specific update.
  • Service providers — email delivery, hosting, and analytics vendors bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal authorities — only where required by law, subpoena, or to protect the safety of donors, volunteers, or field staff.
Section 05

Cookies & Tracking

We use essential cookies to keep your donation session secure, and optional analytics cookies to understand site usage. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings; disabling essential cookies may prevent checkout from functioning correctly.

Section 06

Payment Data

Full card numbers are never stored on Hopewell servers. All payment processing is handled by PCI-DSS-compliant processors, who return a secure token used to reference your payment method for receipts and recurring gifts.

Section 07

Data Retention

Donation and tax-receipt records are retained for as long as required by applicable tax and nonprofit accounting law. Volunteer records are retained for the duration of active participation plus a reasonable period for program history. You may request earlier deletion of non-required data, as described below.

Section 08

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encryption in transit (SSL/TLS), access controls, and regular security reviews, to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your donor account.

Section 09

Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information or update your communication preferences.
  • Request deletion of information not otherwise required for legal or tax purposes.
  • Opt out of non-essential email communications at any time via the unsubscribe link.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@hopewell.org — we respond to verified requests within 30 days.

Section 10

Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Section 11

International Transfers

Hopewell operates in 40 countries. Where personal information is transferred internationally — for example, from a donor in the EU to our servers or field partners elsewhere — we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses to protect that information in transit.

Section 12

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in a new "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Policy periodically.

Section 13

Contact Us

Questions about this Policy or your personal information can be directed to our privacy team.
