Chain of Custody

We believe generosity works best when it's traceable.

Hopewell started in a single village kitchen in 2013 and has grown into a donor network spanning 40 countries — without ever losing the paper trail between your wallet and someone's doorstep.

  • Full transparency

    Track your donation from wallet to warehouse to doorstep.

  • Verified NGO partners

    Every partner is audited annually by an independent body.

  • On-the-ground reach

    Local teams in 40 countries, not a single head office.
