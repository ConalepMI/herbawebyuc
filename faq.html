Donations

Pick a cause from the Causes section, choose one-time or monthly, and complete checkout through our encrypted, PCI-compliant payment form. You'll get an emailed receipt within minutes, and monthly gifts renew automatically until you cancel.

We accept major credit and debit cards, PayPal, Google Pay, and Apple Pay. All transactions are processed through PCI-DSS-compliant payment partners.

Yes — log into your donor account and adjust or cancel your recurring gift anytime, or email hello@hopewell.org and we'll handle it for you within one business day.

No minimum — every gift, however small, is tracked and applied in full to the cause you choose.

If a charge was made in error, contact us within 14 days and we'll review it. Once funds have shipped to a field partner, refunds are reviewed case by case — see our Terms & Conditions for details.

Tax & Receipts

Yes. Hopewell is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and an electronic receipt is generated automatically the moment your donation is confirmed. Consult your tax advisor on your specific eligibility.

Every receipt is stored in your donor account under "Donation History," downloadable as a PDF anytime. You can also request a resend to your registered email.

Yes — a consolidated annual summary of all your donations is emailed each January, ahead of tax season.

Account & Security

Yes. All checkout traffic is SSL-encrypted, and full card numbers are never stored on Hopewell's own servers — only a secure token from our PCI-compliant payment processor.

Use the "Forgot password" link on the account sign-in page — you'll receive a secure reset link by email within a few minutes.

Email hello@hopewell.org and we'll close your account. Past donation and tax records are retained as required by law even after closure.

Causes & Verification

Every partner organization passes an independent annual compliance audit covering financial controls, program delivery, and safeguarding practices before receiving funds through Hopewell.

Yes — your donor dashboard shows real disbursement updates and photos tied to the campaign you supported, tracked from wallet to warehouse to doorstep.

Once a campaign reaches its goal, it closes to new gifts and moves into delivery. Any surplus is redirected to the next related campaign in that same cause category, disclosed on the campaign page.

Volunteering

Register your interest from the Volunteers section. Placement depends on background checks, local partner capacity, and the skills needed on the ground at the time.

Not always — we run remote outreach and logistics roles alongside on-the-ground field placements across our 40 partner countries.

Hopewell volunteer roles are unpaid. Field coordinators provide safety briefings, and select field placements include travel and lodging support — details are shared during onboarding.
